# Yumaxa on-board charger catalogue

Four TLCZ on-board sealed battery charger series (Tianli New Energy brand) and their 24 printed model rows, transcribed from the product tables on the manufacturer's website. Values pending confirmation are left out; where the Chinese and English pages differ, the Chinese value is given.

- **Compiled by:** the website [yumaxa.com](https://yumaxa.com/data/?ref=gh-dataset) (dataset page with field descriptions).
- **Products and data source:** Anhui Tianli New Energy Co., Ltd., the manufacturer — values as listed on its website (each value carries its source URL). Product brand: Tianli New Energy.
- **License: not yet stated by the manufacturer; data as published on its website.**
- **Version:** `5cd86c444e61` (content hash; it changes only when the data changes) · 4 series · 24 printed model rows · 70 model values · 25 series specifications
- **Left out:** 11 values pending confirmation by the manufacturer are left out (9 series specifications, 2 model values); the series pages show them as printed.

## Notes

- 11 values pending confirmation by the manufacturer are left out (9 series specifications, 2 model values); the series pages show them as printed.
- The kW figure in a series name is a series label, not a rated output power.
- A row with two model codes is one printed row; its values are not assigned to either code separately.

## Updates

This repository mirrors the dataset published at https://yumaxa.com/data/. It is regenerated from the live website when the catalogue changes; there are no other commits. For quotations, contact the manufacturer through the website.
